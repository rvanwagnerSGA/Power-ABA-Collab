Renders one of the four approved Power ABA lockups from `assets/logo/`. Never re-typeset or redraw the mark.

```jsx
<Logo lockup="digital" height={52} basePath="../.." href="/" />
```

`digital` for web/social/email, `print` for brochures and apparel, `compact` where horizontal room is tight, `bloom` for the badge alone. Minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the flower.
