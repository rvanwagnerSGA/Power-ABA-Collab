Rounded bubble for short Poppy/Parker dialogue. Keep character dialogue to one line; the full explanation belongs in body copy beside it.

```jsx
<SpeechBubble tone="poppy">Let's find another way!</SpeechBubble>
```

Parker is non-verbal and communicates through an AAC device worn cross-body (casing `--parker-aac` #5FCCC2). When a bubble carries a Parker line, treat it as AAC output, not speech — attribute it to the device in alt text and captions.
