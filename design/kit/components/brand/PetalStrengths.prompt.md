The five Power Bloom petal strengths as labelled chips — for Power Wall Garden displays, Petal Log cards, and clinic-culture materials.

```jsx
<PetalStrengths earned={["courage","kindness"]} />
<PetalStrengths showMeaning layout="stack" />
```

Wording and color mapping are fixed by the Brand Bible. This deliberately does not draw a flower: the Power Bloom artwork must never be reconstructed — pair this with `<PowerBloom>` when a flower is needed.
