# Power Pals social & print templates — UI kit

The recurring asset formats named in the Brand Bible's Master Asset Library, built at their real aspect ratios and scaled down for review.

| Template | Real size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CarouselSlide` | 1080×1080 | Instagram/Facebook carousel — Parker's One-Step Plan, Poppy's Find Another Way, Power Pal Tips. |
| `ReelCover` | 1080×1920 | Reel and Story cover; photo with a navy protection gradient. |
| `BloomFridayCard` | 1080×1080 | Power Bloom Friday quote card, solid red. |
| `ThreeStepGraphic` | 1080×1080 | Three-step tip graphic / printable checklist. |
| `EmailHeader` | 1600×640 | Email header and LinkedIn banner; follows `assets/reference/social-bio-builder-banner.jpg`. |
| `LinkedInCard` | 1200×627 | Professional lane — characters deliberately sit out. |
| `PetalLogCard` | 5×3.5 in | In-clinic take-home Petal Log card. |

## Rules encoded here
- Characters appear on roughly two of every three posts, solo by default; Friday and LinkedIn carry the brand through the Power Bloom mark and color alone.
- One takeaway per asset; the headline speaks to caregivers, character dialogue stays to one line.
- Text over photography always sits on a protection gradient, never directly on the image.
